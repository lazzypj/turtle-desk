Live ticks
Turn on tick-by-tick option prices›
1. Pick a PIN of 6 or more digits.
2. In GitHub: Turtle-strategy › Settings › Secrets and variables › Actions › New repository secret. Name TICK_PIN, value: your PIN.
3. Enter the same PIN above in Connection › Live ticks PIN, then Save.
Each live watch then hands today's Kite login to this app, encrypted with your PIN. The stock you open streams its price and its option chain tick by tick, all day while the app is open, even after the watch ends. The login expires early next morning.
Phone alerts
Set up break alerts on your phone›
1. Install the free ntfy app from the App Store.
2. Pick a private topic name. Use this one, or make your own (it works like a password):
3. In GitHub: Turtle-strategy › Settings › Secrets and variables › Actions › New repository secret. Name NTFY_TOPIC, value: the topic.
4. In the ntfy app, tap + and subscribe to the same topic.
From the next live watch you get one alert when the watch starts, one per fresh break after the quiet time (at most one per stock every 15 minutes), and one when it ends. Tapping an alert opens that stock here.

Kite data is for your own use. Don’t save your token on a shared device.
